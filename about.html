Tradition · Experience · Trust

A traditional approach, built on trust

With 5+ years of experience and 100+ properties personally studied, Shree Kunal Acharya has built his practice on traditional Vāstu Śāstra, practical observation, and the guidance of his family and Guru.

Education & training

His study includes traditional South Indian Vāstu, the sixteen Dishā, Vāstu Padas and Deities, the Vāstu Purusha Mandala, Yantras, traditional remedies and Vāstu rituals, supported by classical texts and practical experience.

From homes and plots to offices, commercial spaces, hotels and industrial properties, his consultations have extended across India and internationally, including Dubai, London and Egypt.

His approach is simple: understand the property before recommending anything. Every area, Kona and the Brahmasthāna is carefully considered before suggesting a correction. Unnecessary demolition or changes are not the first solution; where appropriate, traditional remedies may be considered.

Clients are guided in simple language, without fear or unnecessary pressure.
