What is collected

When you submit the enquiry form on the Contact page, the following is collected, and only because you typed it in yourself:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • City or area
  • What the enquiry is for (residential, commercial, remote, and so on)

Nothing is collected if you only browse the site. Writing directly to hello@shreekunalconsultancy.com instead of using the form is treated the same way: only what you choose to include in that email.

Why it is collected

Solely to reply to your enquiry, understand your property and goals before a first call, and follow up afterwards. It is not used for marketing, and it is not analysed for any purpose beyond responding to you.

Where it is kept

Form submissions are written directly into a private Google Sheet that only Guruji can access. It is not sold, rented, published or shared with any third party, and it is not passed to advertising or analytics services.

Testimonials

A client quote is published on the Testimonials page only after that specific client has seen the exact wording and given permission in writing. Site visits themselves are never described or published; what happens inside a client’s home or business stays with the client.

Third-party services this site uses

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the office location on the Contact page is shown using an embedded Google Map. Both may load resources from Google’s own servers, governed by Google’s privacy policy rather than this one. No advertising or tracking scripts are used on this site.

Questions, corrections or deletion

To ask what is held about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, write to hello@shreekunalconsultancy.com. Requests are handled personally by Guruji, generally within a few days.
