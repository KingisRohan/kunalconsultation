कार्यक्षेत्र

Where the reading is applied

The same eighty-one padas are laid over every plan. What changes from one kind of place to another is which quarter is read most closely, and which devatā is chiefly consulted.

The dwelling

गृह वास्तुGṛha Vāstu

ईशानĪśāna · the quarter chiefly read

Homes, flats and houses, standing or still being raised.

What is read
  • The true north fixed, and the maṇḍala laid over the plan
  • The Brahmasthāna located and read
  • Each room set against the devatā seated in its quarter
  • The doṣas named, in order of weight

The place of trade

वाणिज्य वास्तुVāṇijya Vāstu

उत्तरKubera · the quarter chiefly read

Offices, shops, showrooms and the rooms where accounts are kept.

What is read
  • The entrance and the direction of approach
  • The seat of the one who decides
  • Kubera's quarter and what is stored in it
  • Agni's quarter, where work is transformed

The place of making

उद्योग वास्तुUdyoga Vāstu

आग्नेयAgni · the quarter chiefly read

Workshops, units, warehouses and factories.

What is read
  • Heat, furnace and machinery against Agni's quarter
  • Weight and raw material against Nirṛti
  • The path goods travel through the site
  • The open centre kept open

Examination of the land

भूमि परीक्षाBhūmi Parīkṣā

नैऋत्यNirṛti · the quarter chiefly read

A plot, a flat or a building looked at before it is taken.

What is read
  • Shape, slope and the cut of the corners
  • The roads that meet it and from which side
  • Soil, water and what stood there before
  • Whether it should be taken at all

From the foundation stone

शिलान्यासŚilānyāsa

पूर्वIndra · the quarter chiefly read

Guidance held from first drawing to the day of entering.

What is read
  • Orientation settled before the drawing is fixed
  • The maṇḍala set on the plot itself
  • The foundation rite and its direction
  • Gṛha Praveśa, when the family first enters

Across the waters

विदेश वास्तुVideśa Vāstu

वायव्यVāyu · the quarter chiefly read

For families settled far from where they began.

What is read
  • The plan read from the drawing, wherever it stands
  • True north taken from the family's own reading
  • Guidance spoken over call, in Guruji's voice
  • Remedies chosen from what is at hand in that country

निर्देश

What is given

निर्देशNirdeśa — the guidance, spoken

Guruji gives the correction himself, in his own voice — what stays, what turns, what is placed and in which quarter. Nothing is passed through an assistant and nothing is handed over as a document to be interpreted alone. What is unclear is asked and answered.

Given in Guruji's own voice
Specific to the property
Unclear is asked and answered
Not passed to anyone else

यन्त्र स्थापनYantra Sthāpana — the yantra, prepared

A yantra is drawn for that dwelling alone and for the family living in it, consecrated in Guruji's own practice with the saṅkalpa spoken over it, and sent to the house. It is prepared, never sold as an article.

Drawn for that dwelling alone
Consecrated with saṅkalpa
Sent to the house
Never sold as an article
